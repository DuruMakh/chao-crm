# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Event organizers and catering managers at Chao Catering. They use Chao CRM primarily on mobile devices, often while in active discussion, planning sessions, or directly at event venues. They need to track orders and payments, calculate costs quickly, modify quantities in real-time, and generate client invoices and staff sheets.

## Product Purpose
To track each Chao Catering order from first enquiry through the event, including payments, menus, invoices and staff sheets. Chao CRM stores orders in Google Sheets and uses the existing catalog for new quotes while preserving saved prices. Success means quick updates, reliable saving and clear event preparation.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, Modern, and Technical. 
- **Voice/Tone**: Direct, precise, and professional.
- **3-Word Personality**: Understated, Efficient, Precise.
- **Emotional Goals**: Invites confidence, clarity, and reliability.

## Anti-references
- **Generic SaaS UI Templates**: Avoid default card grids, purple/blue gradients, and saturated cream/beige/sand backgrounds.
- **Cluttered Spreadsheets**: Avoid raw, non-responsive tables that feel like a direct Excel export.
- **Decorative Elements**: Avoid glassmorphic gradients or animations that do not serve utility.

## Design Principles
1. **Utility is Beauty**: Layout and aesthetic decisions must prioritize information density, reading speed, and ease of interaction.
2. **Technical Understatement**: Emphasize clean typography, ample space, and structural borders rather than saturated background fills or rounded card clusters.
3. **Frictionless Real-Time Loop**: Every tap, search, and adjustment should reflect instantly with subtle, high-performance visual feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Contrast**: Maintain strict WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 for body and placeholder text) to support outdoor or low-light use.
- **Touch Targets**: Minimized layout shift and generous touch targets (≥44x44px) for rapid mobile inputs.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respect system preferences; all transitions and animations degrade to static layouts or simple crossfades.
